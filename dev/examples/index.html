Examples

A gallery of examples of the method of Lagrangian descriptors, from simple systems with known phase portraits to chaotic and stochastic ones. Each example sets up the problem, computes the Lagrangian descriptors, and discusses the picture.

ExampleFeatures
Saddle pointlinear system; stable and unstable manifolds
Pendulumconservative system; separatrices between oscillations and rotations
Van der Pol oscillatorlimit cycle; solutions diverging backward in time
Double gyretime-dependent fluid flow; lobes and transport
Lorenz systemthree-dimensional chaotic system; two-dimensional slices of initial conditions
Double pendulumfour-dimensional chaotic Hamiltonian system
Stochastic Duffing equationstochastic differential equation, with multiplicative noise; a single realization and the mean over realizations

The periodically forced Duffing equation, a random Duffing equation and a Duffing equation with additive noise are treated step by step in the tutorials on ODEs, RODEs and SDEs. For the choice of the infinitesimal descriptor, the time window and the grid of initial conditions, see Choosing the descriptor and the time window.
