References

The method of Lagrangian descriptors was introduced by Jiménez Madrid and Mancho (2009) and developed by Mancho et al. (2013), with a theoretical framework given by Lopesino et al. (2017) and an extension to stochastic differential equations by Balibrea-Iniesta et al. (2016). For an introduction to the method, see Wiggins and García-Garrido (2022), and for a comprehensive account, with many examples, see the online book by Agaoglou et al. (2020). Answers to frequently asked questions about the method are collected by Mancho (2016).
